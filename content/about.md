I'm a **software engineer** at Age Bold, where I build **large-scale healthcare systems** that connect Medicare members to preventive care. My work spans data pipelines that ingest and reconcile health plan data at high volume, the ML serving stack that turns that data into real-time, personalized recommendations, and the product features that return member feedback to the models. I completed my Master's in Computer Science at the University of Southern California (USC). My interests lie at the **intersection of machine learning and software engineering**: the work it takes to turn a model into a system that people can depend on.

Previously, during my internship at Dragonfruit AI, I worked on a computer-vision platform, where I improved the system that processes and analyzes real-time alerts. Before that, at SoFi with Galileo Financial Technologies, I worked on payment infrastructure and fraud detection, building a Kubernetes and RabbitMQ-based monitoring system for a service that processes ACH transactions.

I also conducted research at USC's **Integrated Media Systems Center (IMSC)** under Dr. Seon Ho Kim. My focus was computer vision for street monitoring: object detection, counting and multi-object tracking on real-world street-level video, with homeless encampments in Los Angeles as the case study. That work led to two papers, at IEEE ICIP 2023 and BIC 2024.
